OUR STORY

We believe everyday accessories should spark instant joy.

BAGÉRA was born from a simple sketchbook obsession: why are handbags either plain and corporate or prohibitively expensive? We chose neither.

THE BAGÉRA ETHOS

Carry Your Little World.

Inside every woman's handbag is an entire universe: a favourite lip oil, headphones for daydreaming on the train, emergency mints, cinema tickets, and tiny keepsakes.

That's why every BAGÉRA silhouette is designed around lifestyle moments: from spontaneous late-night boba runs with our Cherry Pop Mini Bag to study dates with our Everyday Canvas Tote.

Plus, our viral Sticker Club lets you customise your look every single season without buying a new wardrobe.

WHY WE DO THIS

The Three Studio Promises

Art-Directed & Playful

No boring neutrals. We celebrate cherry reds, soft blush, and warm ivory tones that instantly elevate any everyday outfit.

Honest Pricing (₹50–₹199)

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I care for my patent cherry handbag?

Wipe gently with a soft microfibre cloth dampened with lukewarm water. Store away from direct sunlight in your complimentary dustbag.

Can I stick the stickers directly on the bag?

Yes! Our Sticker Club stickers are waterproof and leave zero residue when removed from patent leather, or you can stick them to luggage charms, notebooks, and laptops.

What is the delivery timeline?

Studio packages are packed within 24 hours and delivered across India within 3–5 working days. Free shipping kicks in on orders above ₹299!
