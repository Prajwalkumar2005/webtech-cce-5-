OUR PHILOSOPHY

Not just a bag. A little piece of you.

We started BAGÉRA because we believe personal style shouldn't cost a fortune. Modern fashion had become either boringly minimal or overwhelmingly expensive.

So we created a brand that feels like swapping accessories with your chicest friend. We craft bags that hold your phone, your cherry lip balm, your favorite book, and your dreams—while leaving room for creative expression through our die-cut sticker club.
